## Known model weaknesses (judge close calls with care)
- **Early-season priors:** this season's data only, so proven premiums (Haaland, Fernandes, Pedro) are pulled towards the price-band average. In a replay of GW2–5, its captain pick (Szoboszlai) lost to yours.
- **One blowout skews a team:** United's attack rating comes from one 5.15-xG game v Ipswich; without it they're below average. Mbeumo and Fernandes look better on the model than they are.
- **Injuries come only through FPL's flags and recent minutes:** no injury history, and international-duty knocks lag.
- **Planned fix (next task):** weight recent games more, damp single-game outliers, and use last-season player *shares* capped by this season's team strength. Then add the lineup/captain replay to the backtest.
